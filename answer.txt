(a) What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)?

Pros: high flexibility and adaptability
      able to deliver software in smaller, more frequent increments

Cons: struggle with scope creep and may lack a long-term vision
      challenging for large projects or when stakeholders are unclear

(b) Explain the role and responsibilities of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.

Scrum Master: focuses on process, facilitating the Scrum process, ensuring the team works efficiently, and removing blockers

Product Owner: focuses on content by managing the product backlog, prioritizing features, and aligning work with business goals
