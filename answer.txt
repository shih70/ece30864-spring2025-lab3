(a) What are some Pros and Cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)?

Pros: high flexibility and adaptability
      able to deliver software in smaller, more frequent increments

Cons: struggle with scope creep and may lack a long-term vision
      challenging for large projects or when stakeholders are unclear

(b) Explain the role and responsibilities of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.

Scrum Master: focuses on process, facilitating the Scrum process, ensuring the team works efficiently, and removing blockers

Product Owner: focuses on content by managing the product backlog, prioritizing features, and aligning work with business goals

(c) What are the 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle? List at least 2 points on what needs to be discussed during each of these meetings.

Sprint Planning: sprint goal, assign stories to team

Daily Scrum: what was done yesterday, what will be done today

Sprint Retrospective: completed work, feedback from stakeholders

(d) Differentiate between Epic, Story, Bug, and Task issue types in JIRA

Epic: large body of work that spans across multiple sprints and is often related to a major feature which can be broken down into smaller tasks or stories

Story: smaller, more detailed unit of work that describes a feature or functionality from the end user's perspective

Bug: represents a defect or problem in the system that needs to be fixed

Task: low-level implementation detail performed by a software developer
